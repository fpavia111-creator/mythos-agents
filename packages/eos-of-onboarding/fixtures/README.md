Fictional people (example.com addresses), roles and sample checklist templates. The templates are examples, not a statement of what any law requires.

- `people.csv`, `status.csv`: five people as of 2026-11-14 (one contractor ended 2026-10-31; `C3,return-laptop` is an offboarding status row).
- `template.json`: checklists per type plus `offboarding` tasks per type.
- `template-roles.json`: the same with a `roles` section (Front-end developer, Copywriter, Account executive, Client); "Designer" is deliberately missing.
- `bad-template-typo.json`: a template with a misspelt key, used by the config-validation eval.
