Fictional employees, merchants, receipts and policy ("Example Co. Travel & Expense Policy v3"). Amounts, per-diem and mileage rates are made up for the demo; they are not a recommended policy and not official rates.

- `expenses.csv`, `policy.json`, `receipts/`: the main review sample.
- `expenses-travel.csv`: per-diem lines by location and mileage lines either side of the mid-year rate change.
- `history.csv`: earlier (already paid) lines for cross-period duplicate checks. `receipts/r090-dinner-scan.txt` is a re-typed copy of `r101-dinner.txt`.
- `receipt-styles/`: five receipt text layouts (US diner, "amount due" invoice, European decimal comma, month-name date in GBP, hotel folio with a grand total in CAD).
- `bad-policy-typo.json`: a policy with a misspelt key, used by the config-validation eval.
