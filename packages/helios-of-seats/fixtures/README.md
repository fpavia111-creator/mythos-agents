Fictional seats, prices, people and contract terms (example.com). Not real pricing.

- `seats-previous.csv`: an earlier export (as of 2026-10-14) for `helios_compare_reviews`.
- `hr-roster.csv`: HR roster with status and manager email (managers go in Cc on owner drafts).
- `helios-policy.json`: contract terms per app (billing, renewal date, notice days, minimum seats, owner).
