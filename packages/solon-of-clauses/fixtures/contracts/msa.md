# Master Services Agreement (fictional)

This Master Services Agreement is between Kettle Labs Ltd ("Supplier") and Fernhill Studio LLC ("Customer"). Both companies are made up for this example.

## 1. Definitions

"Services" means the work described in each statement of work. "Confidential Information" means non-public information a party shares under this Agreement.

## 2. Services

Supplier will perform the Services described in each statement of work with reasonable skill and care.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days of receipt. Late amounts bear interest at 1% per month.

## 4. Term and Renewal

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and lasts one year. It will automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party gives notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the current term.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information with reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

Except for breach of Section 5, in no event will either party's total liability exceed the fees paid in the three (3) months before the claim.

## 7. Indemnification

Supplier will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Services infringe intellectual property rights.

## 8. Data Protection

Where Supplier processes personal data for Customer, the parties will sign a data processing agreement, and Supplier will follow the security measures in Section 12.3.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [●].

## 10. Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in the order form.
